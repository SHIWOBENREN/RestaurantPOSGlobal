# Support / 技术支持

Current status: development and release preparation; there is no generally available production installer and no guaranteed public SLA.

For non-sensitive questions and reproducible bugs, you may use the repository's Issues section if enabled. Include: product version, OS version, whether the desktop host or phone is affected, expected vs. actual behavior, and reproducible steps.

**Never attach** real customer databases, receipt or order exports, passwords, installation IDs, signed license JSONs, setup tokens or private keys to a public Issue.

Paid support scope, purchase contact, business identity, time zone, response times and refund terms **must be supplied by the distributor before any commercial sale**. No support email address or payment method is implied by this repository.
